Daily logic puzzles for iPhone and iPad

Four fresh puzzles every day: Sudoku, Balance, Regions and Path. Each has exactly one solution you can reach by reasoning alone.

Common questions

How do I restore Thinking Cap Pro on a new device?
Open the app, tap Settings (the gear on the Today tab), then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Thinking Cap. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

Does Lifetime work with Family Sharing?
Yes. Family members get Pro after Family Sharing is turned on for purchases.

Why don't my scores show on the leaderboards?
Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app. Only a day's first solve of each daily puzzle counts, and very fast solves aren't submitted.

Does my progress sync between devices?
Not yet. Progress, streaks and stats are stored on each device.
